This is a list of the 360 constituencies represented in the [[House of Representatives (Nigeria)|House of Representatives]] of the [[Nigeria|Federal Republic of Nigeria]], as at November 2024.
{{Horizontal TOC}}

== History ==
The House of Representatives (also called Green Chamber) is the [[Lower house|lower chamber]] of Nigeria's [[Bicameralism|bicameral]] National Assembly.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2019-06-12 |title=Nigeria National Assembly leadership: Ruling APC sweeps all top posts |url=https://www.africanews.com/2019/06/12/security-high-as-nigeria-lawmakers-elect-new-leaders/ |access-date=2024-11-07 |website=Africanews |language=en}}</ref> The Green Chamber has 360 members who are elected in single-member [[Constituency|constituencies]] using the plurality (or [[first-past-the-post]]) system, most recently [[2023 Nigerian House of Representatives election|in 2023]]. Members serve four-year terms.
